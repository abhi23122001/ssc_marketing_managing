## 1. PURPOSE & ARCHITECTURE

The Daily AI Marketing Engine provides Shah Group with a dynamic, highly specialized content engine tailored specifically to:
1. **Shah Survey & Consultants (SSC):** Precision engineering, topographic survey, DGPS RTK, Drone LiDAR, and GIS cartography.
2. **Shah Infrastructure & Construction (INFRA):** Highway civil works, earthworks, MoRTH specifications, concrete paving, and structural EPC.

### Core Distinctions from Generic AI:
- **Zero Generic Answers:** Unlike generic LLMs that produce repetitive social media copy, this engine incorporates real engineering parameters (e.g. sub-centimeter RTK tolerances, point cloud density, MoRTH Section 500 bitumen compaction standards, DTM/DEM cut-and-fill volumetrics).
- **Non-Repeating Pillar Rotation:** Cycles automatically across 14 specialized SSC pillars and 10 INFRA civil engineering pillars, ensuring unique daily angles without repetition.
- **Production-Ready 4-Part Scripts:** Provides ready-to-shoot scripts broken into Hook (0–5s), Problem (5–20s), Solution (20–40s), and Call-to-Action (40–55s).
- **Direct Content Calendar Bridge:** Approved concepts are saved directly as verified drafts into the Content Calendar with a single click.

## 3. 4-PART SCRIPT & VISUAL DIRECTION STRUCTURE

Every generated brief provides practical production instructions:

### 1. Dual-Language Video Script (Hindi & English):
- **Hook (0–5 seconds):** A high-impact opening that arrests scrolling engineers and contractors.
- **Problem (5–20 seconds):** Demonstrates how inaccurate traditional surveys cause budget overruns or NHAI rejections.
- **Solution (20–40 seconds):** Explains Shah Group's specialized DGPS/LiDAR or heavy equipment methodology.
- **Call-to-Action (40–55 seconds):** Clear contact instruction for nationwide tenders and engineering projects.

### 2. Shot-by-Shot Visual Direction:
- **Shot 1 (0–5s):** Wide establishing shot on-site with branded yellow jackets, RTK base station, or drone take-off.
- **Shot 2 (5–20s):** Technical close-up showing laser sensor, DGPS screen showing 0.008m accuracy, or vibrating roller compaction.
- **Shot 3 (20–40s):** Computer monitor screencast displaying processed point clouds in AutoCAD Civil 3D, Global Mapper, or MicroStation.
- **Shot 4 (40–55s):** Team outro with contact telephone, email, and website logo watermark.
