# Shared template — Content QA checklist

Use before final approval. This supplements the repository checklist at
`docs/CONTENT-QA-CHECKLIST.md`.

---

## Business and project facts

- [ ] Service and capability confirmed by SSC.
- [ ] No named client/project/location without written approval.
- [ ] No confidential drawings, coordinates, reports, or personal data.
- [ ] Equipment, software, methods, and specifications verified.
- [ ] Accuracy, tolerance, and measurement claims have evidence and
      technical approval.
- [ ] Certifications, approvals, registrations, and statutory statements
      are current and verified.
- [ ] Testimonials and quotations carry permission.

## Content quality

- [ ] Target audience and objective are clear.
- [ ] Hindi/English natural and professional.
- [ ] Headline and CTA truthful.
- [ ] Keywords relevant and not stuffed.
- [ ] Hashtags relevant; any "trending" claim supported by current research.
- [ ] Images/video owned or permission-cleared.
- [ ] AI-generated illustrative visuals not represented as real evidence.

## Approval and publishing

- [ ] Owner/reviewer approved final text and media.
- [ ] Technical statements have qualified technical review.
- [ ] Platform, account, date, and final published version confirmed.
- [ ] Publishing or outreach performed only after explicit authorization.

## Technical claims requiring qualified review

If any of the following appear, stop and flag for SSC review:

- Survey accuracy, tolerance, or measurement claims
- Drone/aviation compliance or flight permission
- Geotechnical, engineering, or safety statements
- Statutory or legal language
