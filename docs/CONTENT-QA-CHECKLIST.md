# SSC public content review checklist

Use this checklist before approving any post, page, brochure, video or outreach message.

## Business and project facts
- [ ] Service and capability are currently confirmed by SSC.
- [ ] Project/client name and location have written approval for public use.
- [ ] No confidential drawings, coordinates, site data, reports or personal information appear.
- [ ] Equipment, software, methods and specifications are verified.
- [ ] Accuracy, tolerance, measurements and delivery claims have evidence and technical approval.
- [ ] Certifications, approvals, registrations and statutory statements are current and verified.
- [ ] Testimonials, logos and customer quotations have permission.

## Content quality
- [ ] The target audience and objective are clear.
- [ ] Hindi/English is natural and professional.
- [ ] The headline and CTA are truthful.
- [ ] Keywords are relevant and not stuffed.
- [ ] Hashtags are relevant; any claim that they are trending is supported by current research.
- [ ] Images/video are SSC-owned or permission-cleared.
- [ ] AI-generated illustrative visuals are not represented as real survey/project evidence.

## Approval and publishing
- [ ] SSC owner/reviewer has approved the final text and media.
- [ ] Any technical statement has qualified technical review.
- [ ] Platform, account, date and final published version are confirmed.
- [ ] Publishing or outreach is performed only after explicit authorization.
