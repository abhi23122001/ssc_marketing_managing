# Module 04 — LinkedIn B2B Marketing

## Purpose

Professional B2B content for SHAH AI on LinkedIn, aimed at Indian EPC
contractors, construction, infrastructure, mining, power, and solar teams.
The focus is on practical insights, process explanations, and the value of
survey data for planning, measurement, documentation, and site coordination.

## What this module now contains

| File | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `data/linkedin-topics.csv` | LinkedIn topic clusters and service match |
| `data/content-calendar.csv` | Planning calendar (shared) |
| `data/instagram-library.csv` | Cross-platform asset ideas |
| `shared/templates/content-template.md` | Standard content draft template |
| `shared/scripts/Calendar.ps1` | Planning views |

## Content pillars (from `data/linkedin-topics.csv`)

| Topic | Example angle |
|---|---|
| Survey & topography | Planning, measurement, documentation, decision support |
| Drone surveying & photogrammetry | Aerial coverage, mapping, efficiency |
| LiDAR & geospatial workflow | Point clouds, processing, deliverables |
| GPR & geophysical surveys | Subsurface detection, when used |
| Geotechnical & civil infrastructure | Soil, bearing readiness |
| Mining & industrial projects | Site measurement, coordination |
| Power & energy projects | Survey for grid, solar, transmission |
| Solar project contractors | Site measurement and survey support |

## Approval boundary

- No fabricated projects, case outcomes, testimonials, prices, or clients.
- Any accuracy or compliance claim requires qualified SSC review.
- Outreach is drafted only; never sent without explicit approval.

## Alignment with content calendar

Use `data/content-calendar.csv` for dates/platforms and `data/linkedin-topics.csv`
for topics. Keep posts draft-only until the owner approves the date and content.
