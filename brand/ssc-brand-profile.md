## Brand voice
- Professional, technically aware, reliable and practical.
- Clear Indian B2B language; provide Hindi and English versions where useful.
- Explain the business value of survey data: planning, measurement, documentation, decision support and site coordination.
- Avoid exaggerated claims, sensational language and unsupported superlatives.
- Use a confident but respectful tone; keep calls to action specific and low-pressure.

- A client relationship, named project, work order, contract, site visit or project outcome.

- Years of company operation, team size, completed project count, geographic coverage or guaranteed delivery time.

The owner has previously discussed work or opportunities involving organizations such as NTPC, NCL, SECL and Coal India. Do not use these organizations' names, logos or project details in public-facing material as customer proof without explicit owner approval and evidence for the exact claim.
